# Cue

- `cue-video.mp4`: one trial, 15 s, with the same colours, words and tones that `code/grabar.py` gives during a recording. Made with `python code/hacer_video_cue.py` from the times in `config/config.json`.
- During a recording the cues come from `code/grabar.py`, at the exact sample where each phase starts. The tones matter: with the head up the person cannot see the screen.
