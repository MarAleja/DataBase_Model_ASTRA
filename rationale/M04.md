# M03 rationale: removing the 60 Hz noise

I support our choice to clean the signal with seven notch filters at 60 Hz and its harmonics (up to 420 Hz). The electrical lines around us add a hum at 60 Hz, and it was easy to see in our raw recordings. Notch filters are a practical solution: they are simple, they run on the Arduino and in Python with the same code, and the sensor already does the band-pass (40 to 450 Hz), so we did not need another filter. Because every recording uses the same filters, the model always sees clean signals made the same way.

Source: Mewett DT, Nazeran H, Reynolds KJ. Removing power line noise from recorded EMG. Proc. 23rd Annual EMBS International Conference, Istanbul, 2001.
